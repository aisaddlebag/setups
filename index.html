Make a 45-second film in the watercolor style about the coffee farm my family runs. Warm female narrator.

A film takes the agent about 30-60 minutes and a fair amount of tokens. Needs Node 20+, ffmpeg and Python 3.11+ (or uv). Styles are tuned for Claude Opus 5.5; other models may not reproduce them. Default output 1920x1080, 24 fps.
